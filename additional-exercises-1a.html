Complete the sentences with the correct form of the words in brackets.

Example: I hate that this is your last day; you’ll be sorely missed (sore / miss).
1. I hate to be (brutal / honest), but you’re not cut out for this city.
2. The competition to build the new bridge was (fierce / compete).
3. I can’t believe the city replaced the park with a car park. It’s (total / utter) unfair.
4. I think the science fair is going to be (super / interest).
5. That’s the ugliest building I’ve ever seen. It’s (real / real) awful.
6. I hope you’ll be (bliss / happy) with life in your new city.
7. It’s (complete / boil) in the city today. I have to get out of here!
8. If you think rent’s going to be cheaper here, you’ll be (bitter / disappoint).

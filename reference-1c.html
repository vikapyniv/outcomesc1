Passive Structures for Plans and Predictions

Formal and informal ways to report expected developments, planned actions and predictions.

Core pattern

Reporting plans and predictions

We can use certain verb phrases in the passive to talk about plans and predictions. This is especially common in formal communication such as reports and news media.

subject + be + past participle + to-infinitive
be projected toa calculation or informed projection
be intended tothe purpose of a plan or action
be expected towhat people believe will happen
be forecast toa prediction based on available information
be predicted toa stated prediction about the future
The new rail link is projected to be completed by 2035.
It is intended to reduce travel time to and from the airport by 20 minutes.
The cost is expected to increase significantly.
Rental prices are forecast to rise next year.
Visitor numbers are predicted to drop significantly.
Informal English

Be supposed to

In more informal contexts, we can use be supposed to to describe an arranged or expected plan.

subject + be supposed to + infinitive
The renovation is supposed to be completed by the end of the year.
Completed by a future point

The perfect infinitive

Use the perfect infinitive — have + past participle — to emphasize that something will be completed by a particular point in the future.
be expected to have + past participle
Before 2040
The population reaches three million.
→
By 2040
The change is complete.
The population is expected to have reached three million by 2040.
Plans that changed or failed

Past forms for unrealized plans

Use the past tense of be to show that something did not happen, or might not have happened, as originally planned.

subject + was / were + past participle + to-infinitive
Original expectationThe scheme was expected to create thousands of jobs.planned result
Actual resultIt was unsuccessful.did not happen as expected
Original intentionThe new traffic system was intended to ease congestion in the city centre.intended purpose
Actual resultSix months later, there was very little difference.plan was ineffective

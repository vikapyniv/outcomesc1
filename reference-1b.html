Binomials

Fixed pairs of words joined together to form memorable and natural expressions.

Definition

What are binomials?

Binomials are pairs of words usually linked together by and. The words are always used in the same order.

word 1andword 2
The same initial letterThe two main words may begin with the same sound or letter.
Similar soundsThe words may rhyme or contain a repeated sound pattern.
Near synonymsThe words may have similar meanings and reinforce each other.
Connected meaningsThe ideas may naturally belong together.
OppositesThe words may express contrasting directions or ideas.
Remember: a binomial is a fixed expression. Changing the order usually sounds unnatural.
Examples

Binomials in context

bits and pieces

He went to a supermarket to buy a few bits and pieces.

up and down

He was walking up and down the aisles.

Developing conversations

Giving your impression

Useful questions and structures for describing people we have only met a few times or only know by sight.

Asking for an impression

Use these questions when you want to know what someone thinks another person is like.

Question 1What do you make of the guy with the hat?
Question 2What’s your impression of your new boss?

Giving your impression

These four structures help you express an opinion based on someone’s appearance, behaviour or the limited experience you have of them.

seem + adjective

— appear to be; give a particular impression

He seems fairly laid-back.

strike someone as

— make someone think or feel that you are a particular kind of person

She strikes me as someone you can trust.

come across as

— appear to other people in a particular way

He comes across as a real gentleman.

get the impression / feeling

— form a general idea or feeling about someone

I get the impression / feeling she’s very principled.

Remember: these expressions describe an impression, not necessarily a confirmed fact.

Get the impression

Useful phrases for describing the impression someone gives you.

Ready-to-use phrases

Use these expressions when sharing your first impression of someone.

What do you make of the guy with the hat?— What do you think of him?
What’s your impression of your new boss?— What do you think they’re like?
He seems fairly laid-back.— He appears quite relaxed.
She strikes me as someone you can trust.— She gives me that impression.
He comes across as a real gentleman.— He seems like a gentleman.
I get the impression / feeling she’s very principled.— I feel she has strong principles.

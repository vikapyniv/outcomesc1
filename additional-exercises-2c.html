Grammar

Would

Practise sentence transformations, verb forms and matching sentence halves.

1. Complete the second sentence.

Use would and between three and five other words, including the word in bold.

Example: I’m completely with you on that. AGREE
I would completely agree with you about that.

2. Complete with would(n’t) and the correct form.

Complete each sentence using the verb in brackets.

I wish you ’d close (close) your mouth when you eat!

3. Match the sentence halves.

Select a beginning in the left column, then select its ending in the right column.

Example: If you’d told me about the concert, I would have come.

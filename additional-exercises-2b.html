Grammar

Phrasal verbs

Practise phrasal-verb types, particles and word order.

1. Sort the phrasal verbs into four types.

Drag each verb into the correct column, or tap a verb and then tap a column.

2. Complete the sentences using the pairs of particles.

Each word is a separate draggable tile. Drag or tap each particle into a gap.

3. Order the words to make sentences.

Drag the words into the answer area, or tap them in the correct order. Tap a placed word to return it.
